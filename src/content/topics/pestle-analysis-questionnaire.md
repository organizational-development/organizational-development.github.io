# PESTLE Analysis Questionnaire

To run a PESTLE scan that changes behavior rather than producing a document, use
this worksheet. For each factor, record direction, horizon, impact on us (1–5),
and our confidence in the assessment (1–5), plus a named owner.

The exercise earns its keep in Step 8, where high-impact and low-confidence
factors are assigned a watcher, an indicator, and a trigger threshold.

See [pestle-analysis](../pestle-analysis/).

**Evidence: Not an empirical model — a checklist.** The six categories are an
arbitrary but reasonably exhaustive partition, with no validation. The wider
practice of environmental scanning has modest support in the strategy
literature.


## How to use the columns

* **Direction** — which way is it moving, and how fast?
* **Horizon** — when does it bite? Now, 1 year, 3 years, 5+ years.
* **Impact (1–5)** — how much would this affect us if our current view is right?
* **Confidence (1–5)** — how sure are we about the direction and magnitude?
* **Owner** — the named person who watches this. Not a committee.

## Step 10: Analysis

* **Long lists with no priority are the standard failure.** Cap each category at
  three or four factors that genuinely matter.

* **Beware consensus platitudes.** "AI will be important" is not a factor. "Our
  transcription vendor's model will make two FTE roles redundant within 18
  months, and we have no redeployment plan" is.

* **Health care scans are usually Political and Legal dominant**; software scans
  are usually Technological and Legal dominant. If your scan is evenly spread
  across all six, it is probably generic and was not done seriously.

* **Slow, highly predictable factors are the most under-planned.** An ageing
  population, an expiring contract, a known regulatory deadline — high
  confidence, high impact, and routinely ignored because they are not urgent
  yet.

* **PESTLE says nothing about competitors specifically.** Pair with Porter's five
  forces if competitive dynamics are the live question.

* **Feed the output into the diagnostic.** The external environment is the input
  factor in
  [burke-litwin-causal-model-questionnaire](../burke-litwin-causal-model-questionnaire/),
  and it is the term that
  [mckinsey-7s-framework-questionnaire](../mckinsey-7s-framework-questionnaire/)
  omits entirely.
